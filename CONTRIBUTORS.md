# TradeShift — Contributors

TradeShift was developed collaboratively by the following team members. The work was organised across cloud infrastructure and application engineering, international trade data engineering, and economic analysis. Team members also collaborated on integration, testing, documentation, and project review.

## Jaanvi

- AWS cloud infrastructure and deployment configuration, including CloudFormation, EC2, networking, load balancing, auto scaling, monitoring, and supporting deployment scripts.
- Application and backend architecture, system integration, database connectivity, storage integration, and configuration.
- Infrastructure reliability, security configuration, deployment validation, and system-level testing.
- Integration of the data and analytical components into the deployed application.

## Nikhil

- International trade data acquisition and source integration, including US Census, UN Comtrade, and Singapore trade data workflows.
- Data ingestion, cleaning, normalisation, HS6 product classification, country mapping, and pipeline reliability.
- Supplier-level trade analysis, trade explorer, supplier comparison, and top-mover functionality.
- Data quality checks, pipeline testing, and data preparation for economic analysis.

## Aryan

- Tariff-event research, tariff data preparation, and tariff detection methodology.
- Economic analytics, including trade diversion and trade destruction measures, supplier market shares, and market concentration using HHI.
- Difference-in-Differences validation and interpretation of analytical results.
- Singapore trade and re-export analysis, economic interpretation, and business implications.
- Analytical validation, economic test cases, and research findings.

## Shared contributions

All team members contributed to understanding the project objectives, reviewing the integrated platform, discussing design decisions, testing their respective components, reviewing documentation, and preparing for the project demonstration and defence. Cross-workstream changes were coordinated and reviewed collaboratively.
